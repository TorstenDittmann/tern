# Features

Everything Tern does, one line each.

- **Native and lightweight.** Plain AppKit around `WKWebView`: no Electron and no bundled Chromium. The release build is about 3.8 MB.
- **Vertical tabs.** Drag to reorder, middle-click to close, and right-click for pin, duplicate, suspend, or close others. Pinned tabs stay at the top. The tab list sits on the right by default, level with the address bar, and can move to the left (Settings ▸ General, or **Move Tab List to the Left** in ⌘K). It can be dragged as narrow as 100 points; below 170 the bottom bar hides the memory readout. New tabs open with ⌘T.
- **Wide address bar.** Back, forward, reload and the address bar sit in one bar across the top of the page, so long URLs have room. It stays there when the sidebar is hidden.
- **Keyboard tab list (⌘E).** Type to filter the sidebar, ↑↓ to move, ↩ to open, ⌘⌫ to close, esc to go back to the page.
- **Link hints (⌘J).** Every link, button and field on screen gets a short letter code. Type it to click; hold ⇧ on the last letter to open a link in a background tab, or ⌥ to peek at it. A bar in the corner shows what you've typed.
- **Link peek.** ⌥-click a link to open it in a floating preview. ⌘↩ keeps it as a tab, ⇧⌘↩ opens it in a split, esc closes it.
- **Split view.** Two to four tabs side by side, stacked, or in a grid, with draggable dividers. Drag a tab onto the page to split with it, or use the split button at the end of the top bar. Each pane has a small header with its host, and focus follows the pane you click.
- **Command palette (⌘K).** Fuzzy-search open tabs, your history and every command, or type a URL or a search. Pages and commands you use often and recently rank first, and every command shows its shortcut.
- **History and bookmarks.** Visited pages are remembered for ⌘K, and the address bar completes sites as you type. ⌘D (or the star in the address bar) bookmarks a page. ⌘Y and ⌥⌘B open a window to search, open, rename or delete them. New tabs show your bookmarks and most visited sites.
- **!bangs.** Type `!gh swift-nio`, `!mdn grid`, `!npm zod` or `!so` in the address bar or ⌘K to search that site directly; a bang on its own opens the site. About 25 are built in (`!g`, `!ddg`, `!gh`, `!mdn`, `!caniuse`, `!so`, `!npm`, `!pypi`, `!crates`, `!rs`, `!go`, `!spi`, `!apple`, `!docker`, `!hn`, `!r`, `!yt`, `!w`, `!maps`, `!tr`, …). Add your own in `~/.config/tern/bangs.json`, like `{ "jira": "https://acme.atlassian.net/browse/%s" }`.
- **Pinned tabs that go back home.** A pinned tab remembers its page. ⌘W on a pinned tab that has wandered off takes it back there; ⌘W on one that's already home puts it to sleep and shows the previous tab. Right-click ▸ **Make This the Pinned Page** changes where home is.
- **Archive idle tabs.** Settings ▸ General ▸ **Archive idle tabs** closes tabs you haven't opened in 12 hours, a day, a week or 30 days (off by default). Pinned tabs, tabs on screen and tabs playing sound stay. Archived tabs show up when you search in ⌘K, and **Archived Tabs…** lists them all.
- **Compact mode (⇧⌘L), on by default.** The address bar stays hidden so the page reaches the top of the window. Point at the top edge, or press ⌘L, to bring the bar over the page. To keep it showing, press ⇧⌘L or turn on Settings ▸ General ▸ Always show the address bar.
- **Private windows (⇧⌘N).** Tabs in a private window share cookies and site data that live only in memory and are gone when the window closes. They add nothing to history, aren't saved with your session, and extensions can't see them. The top bar shows a **Private** badge.
- **Responsive design mode (⌃⌘R).** Lays the page out at a phone, tablet or laptop size, centered in its card, with that device's user agent and pixel density. The bar above the page picks the device, rotates it, and overrides the user agent (Safari, Chrome or Firefox on Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android).
- **JSON viewer.** JSON responses open as a collapsible, colored tree with expand and collapse all, a raw view that can be formatted, and copy.
- **Reader (⌥⌘R).** Shows just the article in a calm, readable column over the page, in light or dark. Links open in the tab, and the reader closes when you navigate away.
- **Downloads.** A downloads button appears in the top bar once something is downloading. Its popover (⇧⌘J) shows progress, opens finished files, shows them in Finder, and cancels or retries.
- **Print and PDF.** ⌥⌘P prints the page, and **File ▸ Export as PDF…** saves it as a paginated PDF.
- **Mute tabs.** A tab playing sound shows a speaker on its icon; click it, right-click ▸ **Mute Tab**, or press ⇧⌘M.
- **Site sign-in and permissions.** Sites behind HTTP basic or digest auth (staging servers, routers) get a sign-in sheet. Camera, microphone and location requests ask first and can be remembered per site; **Reset Permissions for This Site** in ⌘K forgets them.
- **Default browser.** **Tern ▸ Make Tern the Default Browser**, or the button in Settings ▸ General.
- **Tab picker (⌘P).** Jump between open tabs across all windows, most recently used first.
- **Welcome.** The first launch walks through five short pages: pick the tab list's side, compact mode and the default browser (applied live); practice ⌘K, ⌘P, ⌥⌘→ and ⌘J by pressing them; turn on extensions; and switch on the agent API, with the `claude mcp add` command to copy. ← → or ↩ move between pages, esc skips. **Welcome to Tern** in ⌘K or the Tern menu shows it again.
- **Rebindable shortcuts.** Every command has a binding. Change them in Settings ▸ Shortcuts or in a JSON file. The menus always show your current bindings.
- **Requests (⌥⌘N).** A small window lists every request the tab's page made: status, type, size and time, filtered by type or URL, with failed requests in red. Double-click opens one in a tab; right-click copies the URL or a cURL command. **Preserve log** keeps it across navigations.
- **Built for developers.** Web Inspector and console shortcuts, copy the URL or a Markdown link, and links like `vscode://` and `mailto:` open in their own apps.
- **Extensions.** On a Chrome Web Store extension page, **Add to Tern** beside the address bar installs it. Tern also finds Safari extensions installed with your apps and loads unpacked or `.zip` extensions in place.
- **Private by default.** Ads and trackers are blocked with EasyList and EasyPrivacy, compiled to native WebKit content rules, so blocking costs the page almost nothing. Links lose tracking parameters like `utm_source` and `fbclid` before they load, and third-party pings are dropped. Local dev servers (`localhost`, `*.test`, `*.local`) are left alone. **Toggle Ad Blocking for This Site** in ⌘K turns it off for a site, or on for a dev server, and Settings turns it off everywhere.
- **Memory-friendly tabs.** Background tabs give their memory back after 15 minutes idle, or when macOS runs low on memory, and keep their history. Suspended tabs show a moon in the tab list, and the bottom bar shows how much memory Tern and its pages use.
